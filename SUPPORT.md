# Getting help with drift

## Private help

Email [mike@lab44.dev](mailto:mike@lab44.dev?subject=drift%20support) about your own library, your account, account deletion or anything you'd rather not post publicly. A screenshot and your drift version help a lot. You'll find the version in drift under **settings › about & support**.

The [privacy and support page](https://drift.lab44.dev/privacy) answers common questions and explains what drift stores and where.

## Public bugs and ideas

- **Something broken?** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/IamMikeHelsel/drift-cast/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) with steps to reproduce it.
- **An idea?** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/IamMikeHelsel/drift-cast/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml), or add a 👍 to the first post of an existing request.
- **What's next?** See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and the [feedback board](https://iammikehelsel.github.io/drift-cast/).

Issues here are public. Never post account details, private feed addresses, recordings or credentials. If you're testing a beta, you can also use TestFlight's **Share Beta Feedback**.

For security or privacy vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an issue.
