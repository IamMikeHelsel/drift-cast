# drift roadmap

More listening. Less tending.

This is the public direction for drift. Plans can change; there are no promised dates here. Updated October 12, 2026.

| Status | Focus | What success looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shipped | drift for iPhone and iPad | The app is available on the App Store. |
| In progress | Default dark app icon | The App Store and a fresh install show the intended dark icon. |
| In progress | Feedback and roadmap | Report a bug, suggest a feature, and follow decisions here. |
| Considering | Less queue management | More useful automatic ordering while keeping your choices in control. |
| Considering | More useful reading and listening tools | Better ways to work with articles, transcripts, and saved material. |
| Preparing private tests | Optional paid capabilities | Try the benefits and ownership experience before offering anything for sale. No new paid offering is announced. |

## Help guide it

[Report a bug](https://github.com/IamMikeHelsel/drift-feedback/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml) or [suggest a feature](https://github.com/IamMikeHelsel/drift-feedback/issues/new?template=feature-request.yml). Tell us what you are trying to do and what gets in the way. Add a thumbs-up to an existing request when it describes your need.

Votes help set priorities. Reliability, accessibility, and the usefulness of a change matter too. A request marked planned is an intention, not a delivery commitment. Shipped means available to App Store listeners; a private test does not count.

Your reports here are public. For account details or other private support, use [drift support](https://drift.lab44.dev/privacy).
